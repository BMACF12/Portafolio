## Context

Mobile e-commerce application for small businesses that need to sell without investing in complex infrastructure.

## Technical challenges

- Persistent cart that survives app closures and connection loss.
- Stripe payments meeting security requirements.
- Smooth performance on low-end devices (60fps on long lists).

## What I learned

The BLoC architecture completely separates business logic from the UI, making it easy to test each purchase flow in isolation.
